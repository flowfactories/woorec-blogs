## The Personalization Crisis in B2B Ecommerce

For **GiftCraft Corporate**, scaling to **$75,000 monthly GMV** exposed a critical flaw: **long customization periods for personalized corporate gifts causing peak-season delays**. Their small team struggled to personalize recommendations for diverse corporate recipients without sufficient historical data. 

This led to **high cart abandonment** and suboptimal **Average Order Value (AOV)**. Every visitor was precious with their **limited traffic and high acquisition costs**. Manual rules couldn't handle the complexity of their **WooCommerce** storefront, threatening their survival in a competitive market.

## From Static Rules to AI-Powered Personalization

GiftCraft deployed **WooRec**, a leading **AI Product Recommendation Engine**, as a **SaaS plugin**. This provided immediate speed without technical overhead. The transformation followed a phased architecture evolution:

### Phase 1: Expanding Discovery with Hybrid Recall
*Powered by WooRec Strategy Module*

We replaced static lists with **dynamic recall mechanisms**:

*   **Cold Start Solution**: Implemented **Hot Retrieval** to surface trending gifts for new visitors.
*   **Semantic Matching**: Deployed **Tag-Based Filtering** aligned with corporate gift categories (employee recognition, client appreciation).
*   **The Logic**: Weighted tags (e.g., "premium," "bulk-eligible") automatically matched user profiles to relevant products. This captured latent interests beyond basic keywords.

### Phase 2: The Model Evolution (LR to DeepFM)
*Powered by WooRec Model Serving*

To achieve the **83% B2B Conversion Rate** uplift, we iterated through three ranking stages:

1.  **Baseline (Logistic Regression)**: Initial implementation handled basic feature scoring but missed complex interactions.
2.  **Upgrade (DeepFM)**: Integrated **Deep Factorization Machines** to model high-order feature interactions in their sparse gift catalog data. This dramatically improved relevance.
3.  **Optimization**: For their SaaS deployment, **DeepFM** provided the ideal balance of accuracy and inference speed without requiring deep learning infrastructure.

### Phase 3: Traffic Control for Profitability
*Powered by WooRec Rule Engine*

Raw AI scores needed business alignment:

*   **Diversity (Scatter/Shuffle)**: Prevented category fatigue with a "max 2 per category" rule.
*   **Business Injection**: Reserved slots for high-margin house brands (e.g., Position 4, 10).
*   **Dynamic Weighting**: Boosted **inventory-rich items** during peak seasons and **high-margin products** to maximize profit per order.
